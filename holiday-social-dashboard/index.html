How to read these numbers
  • Views count every time a post was shown, including repeats. Reach counts distinct accounts.
  • Engagement adds reactions, comments and shares. On giveaway posts, entering means commenting and sharing, so these posts overstate organic love for the brand.
  • Link clicks only count clicks that leave the ad. Other ad clicks include likes and expands.
  • Landing page views are Meta’s count of people who loaded the page. GA4 sessions are a separate count, which is why the two can differ.
  • TikTok figures stop where Mia’s sync stopped, and TikTok was run for engagement, so it records no clicks. Organic TikTok isn’t connected.
  • Facebook and Instagram ‘typical’ means the median post. Website totals cover slightly different end dates (21 and 23 Nov).
